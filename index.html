The Story

A kitchen recipe, kept the way it was learned.

Didiko Achar started the way most good achar does — in a home kitchen, in small jars, shared with neighbours and family in Abu Dhabi. Mustard seed, dried red chili, garlic and mustard oil, worked in by hand and left to settle into flavor.

Every jar is made to order in small batches, so what reaches you is close to what would come out of a home kitchen in Nepal — not a factory line.
